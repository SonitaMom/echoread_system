Quick start

  1. Open the document from your library.
  2. Press Play to listen to the page.
  3. Press Back 10 seconds or Forward 10 seconds to move around.
  4. Press Next page to go to the next page.
  5. Have a questions? Use Ask AI.

Listening to a document

  • Play starts reading. It becomes Pause while reading.
  • Stop ends reading and goes back to the start of the page.
  • Back 10 seconds and Forward 10 seconds move inside the page.
  • Open Voice settings to change speed, language, and voice.
  • Download fulll document as MP3 saves the whole document as audio.

Bookmarks

A bookmark saves your place so you can come back later.

  1. Type a name for the page, for example "History of LLM".
  2. Press Add Bookmark.
  3. Later, choose it from Go to a saved bookmark.
  4. To remove one, open Delete a bookmark and confirm.

Ask AI

Ask a question about document, and the Ai answers using its text.

  1. Tpye your question.
  2. Press Ask AI.
  3. The answer is read to you and saved in your Notes.

Notes

Press Add note to write your own note. AI answers are added here automatically.

Fix the text

If the voice reads something wrong, the text may have mistakes. Press Edit extreacted text to fix it.

  • Save changes keeps your edits.
  • Clean formatting with Ai fixes messy spacing and line breaks.
  • Normal Scan is fast and best for typed PDF files.
  • Hard Scan (OCR) is for photos or scanned pages.
  • Ai Sacn is best for difficult pages and Khmer text.
  • Scanning again replaces your current text, including edits.

Language

Use to EN and ខ្មែរ button at the top of every pages to change the language of EchoRead. To change the language of the voice, open Voice settings on the reader page.

Descreased speed Ctrl + ArrowDown
